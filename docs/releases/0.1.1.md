The image now carries StrmQt 0.9.2-headless.1, an **experimental** build of StrmQt's `headless` branch: StrmQt 0.9.2 plus `strmqt admin` and the foreground host.

StrmQt 0.9.2 lets TVs watching the host together play in step: two rooms sound as one, with no echo. It needs StrmLight 0.9.2 or newer on every TV (0.9.3 recommended), and UDP 8339, inside StrmQt's port block, reachable from the TVs. With `network_mode: host`, as in the example [compose.yaml](../../compose.yaml), it is.

`strmqtctl update` moves an installed host to this image. Nothing else changes.
