The first strmqt-headless: a StrmQt streaming host with nobody at its screen, in Docker or
on bare metal, and `strmqtctl` to look after it from your own computer.

- **`strmqtctl`**, for your computer: targets are a machine (this one, or one reached with
  your own `ssh`) and an installation method, Docker or bare metal. `install`, `status`,
  `pair`, `devices`, `unpair`, `stream`, `logs`, `start`/`stop`/`restart`, `update`,
  `uninstall` and `shell` are the same for every target.
- **YouTube Music on a host**: `strmqtctl youtube-music import` reads the sign-in of a
  Firefox profile on your computer and sends only its youtube.com and google.com cookies
  to the host.
- **The image**, `ghcr.io/mpengellyca/strmqt-headless`: Ubuntu 26.04 with StrmQt's and
  Sunshine's release packages, KWin, PipeWire, and StrmQt running its host in the
  foreground. An example [compose.yaml](../../compose.yaml) is the file `install` writes.

Needs StrmQt 0.9.2 or newer on bare metal (it brought `strmqt admin`), and a GPU either
way.
